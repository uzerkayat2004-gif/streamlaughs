## Full description

Your stream. Your people. Your laugh track.

StreamLaughs adds a small, friendly reaction panel to Twitch. Catch your laughs and reaction words, see anonymous fan reactions live, then keep a personal recap of the moments that made you smile.

- Free to use. Pin the smile, open Twitch and press start.
- Local laugh and reaction-word detection, a mic check, sensitivity control and a “not me” correction for a mistaken laugh.
- Anonymous fan reactions and approved words appear only when at least three anonymous contributors qualify. Sharing is optional and off by default; watch-only still connects to the fan service.
- Personal recaps, saved stream history and links back to your moments. Twitch VODs and clips support personal replay sessions; replays do not share live reactions.
- An optional streamer dashboard shows eligible anonymous aggregates for your own channel. No viewer identities or recordings.
- Audio is processed on your PC and never saved or sent. Models are bundled. Raw transcripts are discarded after processing; a private troubleshooting view can show them briefly while open.
- Save or load a local history file when switching installs. Nothing is uploaded. Backups include channels and viewing times, so keep them private.

Store updates install automatically. Microphone permission is requested when you choose to listen. Pause, stop, turn sharing off or delete local data whenever you want.

Detection is an estimate and can make mistakes. Headphones and quiet calibration help. StreamLaughs is independent of Twitch and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Twitch.
