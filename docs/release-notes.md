# StreamLaughs v0.6.1

see who's laughing with you.

- Responsive microphone reactions with isolated speech decoding, bounded mic gain and a segment-only playback guard.
- Welcome mic level check and hidden local diagnostics (settings: Shift-click version).
- Local reactions, anonymous live crowd and Twitch-owner moment stories.
- Audio is processed on your PC and never saved or sent. Sharing is optional.

Download the universal ZIP from the website, extract it, enable Developer mode on your browser’s extensions page, then Load unpacked from the folder containing manifest.json. Pin the icon, finish Welcome and press start on a Twitch stream. Replace that folder’s contents and reload for updates.
