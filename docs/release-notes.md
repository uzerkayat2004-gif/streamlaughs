# StreamLaughs v0.8.0

see who's laughing with you.

- New laughing-face icon and a compact frosted-glass Twitch panel, with drag memory, live bars and a four-second fade.
- Interactive popup, saved-stream actions and unread badges, colourful history grouped by streamer, and optional cached public profile photos and replays.
- Yellow, coral and teal recap stories from real saved moments, plus a locally drawn 1080x1920 PNG share sheet: preview, download, copy and native file sharing where supported.
- Store installs retain browser-managed updates; ZIP installs retain the popup update flow.
- Store submission ZIP contains one manifest.json, no manifest key and no downloads permission; ZIP installs keep self-updates.
- Recaps and settings stay in your browser. Microphone checks, hidden pipeline diagnostics and all local models remain bundled.
- Audio is processed on your PC and never saved or sent. Sharing is optional.

First install: download the universal ZIP from the website, extract it, enable Developer mode on your browser’s extensions page, then Load unpacked from the folder containing manifest.json. Pin the icon, finish Welcome and press start on a Twitch stream. Future updates: use the popup, copy the new files into the same existing folder, then press finish update. After reloading the extension, refresh any open Twitch tab. Never click Remove: keep your saved recaps and settings.
